    a-b
    Fixed start and end.
    a..b
    A window: opens at a, due by b.
    sunset±offset
    Follows sunset in Seoul, with an optional offset.
    !
    Keepout: locks you out while this item is due.
    a-b free time
    No ` ! ` = a normal block, no lock.
    until
    What unlocks it: done, wake, photo, or away 30m.
    min 20m
    The shortest lock, counted from the start.

    For now, Done also acknowledges wake, photo and away items. Proofs will come later.
